FAQ

What kind of businesses do you build for?

Any business that talks to customers, such as shops, clinics, coaching institutes, restaurants, salons, real estate and service providers. Tell us what you do on WhatsApp and we build for it.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

None at all. You message us and we do everything else. Your agent works inside WhatsApp, so there is nothing new to learn.

How long does it take to build?

Once we understand what you need, most agents go live within a few days. More complex ones take a little longer, and we tell you upfront.

What do I pay and how?

A one time setup fee and a simple monthly fee after that. We confirm the exact amount on WhatsApp based on what you need. You pay monthly by UPI or card.

Will it use my own WhatsApp number?

Yes. Your agent runs on your own business WhatsApp number, verified properly. Customers message the same number they always have.

What if I want to change something later?

Just tell us. Changes to your agent, such as new prices, new answers or new flows, are part of the monthly service.
